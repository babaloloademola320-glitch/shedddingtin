Privacy Policy.

Last updated: July 2026

Who We Are

SHEDDINGTIN ("we", "us", "our") operates fully furnished rental properties, hospitality rooms, and a healthcare housing division across the United Kingdom and Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it's handled. For any question about this policy, contact us at hello@sheddingtin.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through this site, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your full name and email address
  • The division you're interested in (Rentals, Hospitality, or Health Care)
  • Any message or details you choose to share

We do not collect payment information through this website. Any deposits, fees, or bookings are handled directly and separately once an inquiry has been discussed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss availability, pricing, or booking details
  • Send relevant updates about the property, room, or service you enquired about
  • Improve how we communicate with prospective clients

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Marketing Communications & Your Consent

When you submit an enquiry form, you'll be asked to explicitly agree before we add you to our contact list and send you follow-up communications beyond a direct reply to your enquiry. We only send these communications with your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing hello@sheddingtin.com or using the unsubscribe link included in any marketing email you receive from us.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party tools to manage communication with you, including email service providers for sending updates you've requested. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform that function, and are bound by their own data protection obligations.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and, where a booking or lease proceeds, for the duration of that relationship plus a reasonable period for record-keeping.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're based, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information — including under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) for clients based in the UK, and the Nigeria Data Protection Act for clients based in Nigeria. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@sheddingtin.com.

Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or applicable law changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled can be sent to hello@sheddingtin.com or via WhatsApp.
